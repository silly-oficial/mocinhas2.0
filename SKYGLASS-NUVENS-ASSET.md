Prompt final usado:

Create an original reusable cloud lettering sprite atlas for a website, inspired by the two attached cloud typography reference photos (not the video UI screenshot). Photorealistic fluffy white cumulus cloud letters, volumetric billowing lobes, fine mist fringes, subtle pale blue-grey self-shadows and bright white sunlit crowns. Output a square transparent PNG, no sky background. Strict uniform 6 columns by 6 rows grid, each cell identical size, one centered glyph per cell, all glyphs same cap height and thick readable bold upright shape. Invisible grid, NO drawn grid lines, no captions, no logos. Each glyph must fit fully inside its cell with 12 percent empty transparent padding on all sides. Exact rows, from left to right: row 1 A B C D E F; row 2 G H I J K L; row 3 M N O P Q R; row 4 S T U V W X; row 5 Y Z 0 1 2 3; row 6 4 5 6 7 8 9. 36 separate glyphs total. All letters are formed entirely of dense realistic puffy clouds like the FONT and SKY HAS NO LIMIT references. They are individual character assets, NOT a word or poster. Maintain mathematically regular cell placement so this image can be sampled as a sprite sheet.
